## KQ1. Oxytocin deficiency as a new endocrine entity: groups, trials, neurophysin-I, diagnostics, population size

### Takeaway
Oxytocin deficiency is being formalized as a diagnosable neuroendocrine entity almost entirely by two groups (University Hospital Basel / Mirjam Christ-Crain and MGH / Elizabeth Lawson), with a dense cluster of readouts between late 2026 and late 2027 (OxyPLEASURE Dec 2026, PHOENIX Feb 2027, OxyMENO mid-2027, FOCUS, OxyTUTION). Neurophysin-I is emerging as the practical biomarker, and the field is actively hunting a safe (non-MDMA) stimulation test; consumer-facing content is essentially limited to a UK Pituitary Foundation recruitment post and university press releases.

### Cited Findings
**Foundational evidence**
- The Basel group (Atila et al., Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology 2023) used MDMA as a provocation test and showed a clinically relevant oxytocin deficiency in patients with arginine vasopressin deficiency (AVP-D, formerly central diabetes insipidus); oxytocin levels rose ~8.5x in healthy controls after MDMA but did not change in AVP-D patients — [University of Basel press release](https://unibas.ch/en/News-Events/News/Uni-Research/Clinically-relevant-deficiency-of-oxytocin.html); [Atila et al. 2023 PDF](https://uni-freiburg.de/bioklin/wp-content/uploads/sites/337/Atila-et-al.-2023-The-Lancet-Diabetes-Endocrinology-Oxytocin-in-response-to-MDMA-provocation-test-in-patients-with-arginine-vasopressin-deficiency.pdf); [idw](https://idw-online.de/de/news814292)
- Even with vasopressin replacement, many AVP-D patients report anxiety, trouble with social interactions and impaired emotional awareness — symptoms attributed to possible oxytocin deficiency — [Technology Networks](https://www.technologynetworks.com/proteomics/news/lack-of-oxytocin-proved-in-people-with-a-vasopressin-deficiency-373297)
- Lawson (MGH) has identified a possible oxytocin-deficient state in hypopituitarism characterized by increased psychopathology, raising the question of oxytocin replacement; her 2025 Endocrine Reviews paper "The oxytocin system and implications for oxytocin deficiency in hypothalamic-pituitary disease" (doi 10.1210/endrev/bnaf008) frames the field — [Endocrine Reviews](https://www.endocrine.org/journals/endocrine-reviews/oxt-system-and-oxytocin-deficiency-in-hpd); [ASCI profile](https://the-asci.org/controllers/asci/AsciProfileController.php?pid=501730)
- A 2025 review ("Oxytocin: a neglected hormone in pituitary disease") states oxytocin deficiency is "a recently identified neuroendocrine entity associated with anxiety and reduced prosocial behavior," with accurate measurement being the major challenge — [PMC12668196](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12668196/)
- Society for Endocrinology ran a clinician-facing feature "Oxytocin deficiency in central diabetes insipidus (AVP deficiency)" in The Endocrinologist, Autumn 2023 — [endocrinology.org](https://www.endocrinology.org/endocrinologist/149-autumn-23/features/oxytocin-deficiency-in-central-diabetes-insipidus-avp-deficiency/)
- Christ-Crain received the 2026 Cloëtta Prize for medical research — [University of Basel](https://www.unibas.ch/en/News-Events/Awards-Honors/Article/Mirjam-Christ-Crain-receives-the-Cloetta-Prize-2026.html)

**Basel trials**
- OxyTUTION (NCT06036004): randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind trial of oxytocin substitution therapy for psychological symptoms in AVP-D; planned 112 participants, University Hospital Basel — [DKF Basel](https://dkf.unibas.ch/en/research/oxytuition-trial/); [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06036004)
- PHOENIX (NCT07361263): "Plasma Oxytocin in Response to Oral Estradiol Valerate and Ethinylestradiol in Healthy Controls and Patients With AVP-Deficiency"; aim is to test whether oral estrogens stimulate oxytocin and neurophysin-1 release so they can serve as a safe diagnostic stimulation test for oxytocin deficiency; recruiting; 28 participants aged 18–50; estimated primary completion February 2027 — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07361263); [decentrialz](https://decentrialz.com/clinical-trials/plasma-oxytocin-response-oral-estrogens-healthy-07361263)
- OxyPLEASURE (NCT06808516): double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial of intranasal oxytocin (24 IU vs saline) on sexual well-being in AVP-D and healthy controls; 42 participants; started July 1, 2025; primary completion December 2026; Part A = 7-day treatment in stable partnerships, Part B = single dose on arousal, fear, empathy — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06808516); [tundraspace](https://tundraspace.com/directory/studies/nct06808516)
- The UK Pituitary Foundation published a patient-facing "Research opportunity: OxyPLEASURE study" post explaining that AVP-D patients may have anxiety, low mood, social withdrawal and reduced quality of life due to oxytocin deficiency — [pituitary.org.uk](https://www.pituitary.org.uk/research-opportunity-oxypleasure-study/)
- OxyMENO (NCT07742124): "Circulating Oxytocin and Its Clinical Correlates in Postmenopausal Women Compared With Premenopausal Controls"; Basel; started August 20, 2026; primary completion est. June 1, 2027, overall Sept 1, 2027; recruiting; primary hypothesis that cumulative neurophysin-I (AUC over one month) is lower in postmenopausal women — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07742124); [medpath](https://trial.medpath.com/clinical-trial/ab3b1104006d5814/nct07742124-circulating-oxytocin-postmenopausal-women)

**MGH / Tonix trial**
- FOCUS (NCT04789148): investigator-initiated (PI Elizabeth Lawson, MGH) randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover pilot of Tonix's intranasal potentiated oxytocin in adult AVP-D; compares single doses of 6 IU (TNX-2900) and 24 IU (TNX-1900) on anxiety, depression and socioemotional markers, with an exploratory 2-week replacement arm; first patient dosed announced October 22, 2025 — [Tonix press release via Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/35605030/tonix-pharmaceuticals-announces-first-patient-dosed-in-the-investigator-initiated-focus-study-of-tonixs-intranasal-potentiated-oxytocin-in-avp-d-conducted-by-massachusetts-general-hospital); [Tonix 8-K Oct 2025](https://ir.tonixpharma.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001999371-25-015796/tnxp_8k-102225.htm)

**Neurophysin-I biomarker**
- Neurophysin I is co-released equimolarly with oxytocin from the same precursor, has a longer half-life, circulates at higher concentrations and can be measured by sandwich immunoassay; MDMA-stimulated neurophysin-I separated oxytocin-deficient patients from controls with ROC-AUC of 100% (conference abstract) — [Endocrine Abstracts vol 110, P861](https://www.endocrine-abstracts.org/ea/0110/ea0110p861); [PMC12668196](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12668196/)
- University of Arizona (MacLean lab) published "Neurophysin I is an analytically robust surrogate biomarker for oxytocin" (2024) — [Arizona Experts](https://experts.arizona.edu/en/publications/neurophysin-i-is-an-analytically-robust-surrogate-biomarker-for-o/); [PDF](https://dogs.arizona.edu/sites/default/files/2026-06/MacLean_2024.pdf)

**Craniopharyngioma / hypopituitarism**
- Systematic review (Jan 2025) of the oxytocin system in craniopharyngioma: incidence 0.5–2 per million per year; patients show blunted salivary oxytocin response to exercise, associated with greater state anxiety and higher BMI; studies giving exogenous oxytocin are "sparse and do not meet required standards" — [PMC11904121](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11904121/)
- Review "Oxytocin therapy in hypopituitarism: challenges and opportunities" (Bangor University) notes possible cognitive-empathy deficits and metabolic disturbances that oxytocin may improve, but unresolved measurement, diagnosis, route and long-term safety questions — [Bangor research portal](https://research.bangor.ac.uk/portal/en/researchoutputs/oxytocin-therapy-in-hypopituitarism(d61ece5b-2d9d-40dc-beee-89dcacb1f8b1)/export.html)

**Population sizes**
- Hypopituitarism: annual incidence 2.1–4.2 per 100,000; prevalence 37–45 per 100,000 — [Endocrinología, Diabetes y Nutrición (2025)](https://www.elsevier.es/en-revista-endocrinologia-diabetes-nutricion-english-ed--413-articulo-analysis-pituitary-dysfunction-in-patients-S2530018025001143)
- AVP deficiency: ~1 in 25,000 (≈4 per 100,000) in UK paediatric guidance; US prevalence cited as 3 per 100,000; AVP-D present in 18.4% of pituitary-tumour patients in a systematic review — [BSPED AVP deficiency 2025](https://www.bsped.org.uk/media/g0zl2ihj/arginine-vasopressin-deficiency-2025.pdf); [AMBOSS](https://knowledge.manus.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Arginine_vasopressin_disorders); [Endocrinología 2025](https://www.elsevier.es/en-revista-endocrinologia-diabetes-nutricion-english-ed--413-articulo-analysis-pituitary-dysfunction-in-patients-S2530018025001143)
- PWS (another oxytocin-neuron-deficient population): 1 in 10,000–30,000 births — [Tonix](https://www.tonixpharma.com/tnx-2900/)

### Inferences
- Applying 3–4 per 100,000 to the US population gives roughly 10,000–14,000 AVP-D patients in the US, and 37–45 per 100,000 gives roughly 125,000–150,000 hypopituitarism patients; the "addressable" oxytocin-deficiency population is therefore small but highly motivated, and widens considerably if the OxyMENO hypothesis (lower NP-I after menopause) holds.
- The PHOENIX design (oral estrogens as a stimulation test) signals that Basel considers MDMA unviable as a routine clinical test and is building toward a clinic-usable diagnostic protocol; a positive PHOENIX readout (Feb 2027) plus a validated NP-I assay would be the catalyst that turns "oxytocin deficiency" from a research concept into something a patient can ask their endocrinologist to test for.
- Between December 2026 and late 2027, there are at least five readouts from two institutions (OxyPLEASURE, PHOENIX, OxyMENO, FOCUS, and potentially OxyTUTION), which is an unusually concentrated news cycle for a topic with almost no lay coverage.
- Lay-facing coverage currently consists of university press releases (2023), one UK charity recruitment post, and one Swiss public lecture ("Drinking and cuddling", Christ-Crain — [museumspass.com](https://www.museumspass.com/en/event/ngib-lecture-drinking-and-cuddling-invisible-influence-hormones)); there is no US patient explainer surfaced in search.

### Gaps
- No source surfaced showing a commercial neurophysin-I diagnostic test in development (no company, CE-mark or LDT announcement found). The Basel/Arizona assays appear to be research-use; UNVERIFIED whether a commercial partner exists.
- OxyTUTION (NCT06036004) current status, start date and estimated completion were not confirmed from snippets.
- Post-bariatric oxytocin deficiency: not researched (search budget exhausted); no evidence located either way.
- ENDO 2026 / ECE 2026 oxytocin-deficiency abstracts were not retrieved; the Endocrine Abstracts vol 110 item is undated in snippet form.
- Whether Lawson's group has a dedicated oxytocin-deficiency diagnostic study beyond FOCUS was not confirmed.

## KQ2. Tinnitus: Biohaven BHV-1955, the NYU trial, the patent, patient-community awareness

### Takeaway
Tinnitus is the clearest case of a large population (tens of millions of US adults; exact figure not verified here) with a newly industry-backed oxytocin program (Biohaven BHV-1955, preclinical, long-acting intranasal) and virtually no patient-facing explanation; the entire published human evidence base is one small 2017 Brazilian pilot and a terminated NYU trial.

### Cited Findings
- BHV-1955 is described in Biohaven filings as "a novel, long-acting intranasal formulation of oxytocin, for the central treatment of tinnitus," targeting the oxytocin receptor centrally; Biohaven acquired oxytocin derivatives for tinnitus through a "Maskbegone Agreement" with an upfront payment in Q1 2026 (per search summary of the 10-Q for the period ended June 30, 2026; full text could not be fetched) — [Biohaven 10-Q FY2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001935979/000193597926000066/bhvn-20260630.htm); [Biohaven R&D Day 8-K 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001935979/000193597926000048/bhvnbiohaven2026rddaynot.htm); [Biohaven pipeline](https://www.biohaven.com/pipeline/)
- BHV-1955 is listed in the preclinical portion of Biohaven's pipeline; Q1 2026 results materials emphasize other programs and contain no clinical milestone for BHV-1955 — [Biohaven Q1 2026 results](https://www.streetinsider.com/PRNewswire/Biohaven+Reports+Recent+Business+Developments+and+First+Quarter+2026+Financial+Results/26425283.html)
- NCT04210310 (NYU Langone), "High Dose Oxytocin Nasal Spray for Treatment of Tinnitus," pilot proof-of-concept placebo-controlled study; started January 15, 2020; primary completion May 10, 2022; terminated, reason "PI departure from the institution" — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04210310); [protocol PDF](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/10/NCT04210310/Prot_SAP_000.pdf)
- Press coverage (2020) described the NYU trial as 30 patients using oxytocin or placebo spray four times daily for six months — [Irish News](https://irishnews.com/lifestyle/2020/11/12/news/nasal-spray-to-silence-that-ringing-in-your-ear-2124421)
- US Patent 11,241,477, "Oxytocin compositions for treatment of tinnitus," exists — [USPTO](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/11241477) (inventor names not visible in snippet; attribution to Newman/Cramer UNVERIFIED)
- Only published human efficacy data: Azevedo et al., Frontiers in Neurology 2017 (Federal University of São Paulo) — Study 1: 15 patients, 10 weeks open-label; Study 2: 16 patients, placebo-controlled crossover; significant decreases in tinnitus sensation on multiple scales; authors call for larger controlled studies — [PMC5613090](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5613090/); [Frontiers](https://frontiersin.org/journals/neurology/articles/10.3389/fneur.2017.00494/full)

### Inferences
- BHV-1955 is preclinical in 2026; a reasonable expectation is IND/Phase 1 in 2027 with first human data no earlier than 2028, which puts a tinnitus-oxytocin news cycle squarely inside the 12–36 month window but with no near-term efficacy readout.
- A "long-acting" formulation implies Biohaven is addressing the dosing-frequency problem seen in the NYU design (four times daily); formulation science rather than new biology is the differentiator.
- Patient-community search queries ("tinnitustalk oxytocin") returned no forum threads in results, suggesting low existing community awareness, though this is weak (absence-of-evidence) signal.

### Gaps
- The May 2026 Lawrence Newman presentation of BHV-1955 was NOT verified by any surfaced source; treat as UNVERIFIED.
- Identity of "Maskbegone" (counterparty), deal terms, milestones and IND timing could not be read because sec.gov fetch was blocked.
- TinnitusTalk content: a domain-restricted search returned nothing; no direct evidence of what the community knows.
- US tinnitus prevalence figure not retrieved (search budget exhausted); commonly cited ~10% of adults is UNVERIFIED here.
- No other active oxytocin-tinnitus trial was found besides the terminated NYU study.

## KQ3. Prader-Willi syndrome: TNX-2900, the carbetocin failure, the PWS information ecosystem

### Takeaway
PWS is the one oxytocin indication with a mature, sophisticated patient information ecosystem (FPWR, Prader-Willi News, PWSA) that already covers trial news in near-real time, so the gap is for lay mechanistic explanation (why carbetocin failed; why magnesium-potentiated oxytocin at low dose might differ), not for news.

### Cited Findings
- TNX-2900 (intranasal potentiated oxytocin) Phase 2 in PWS has been pushed to Q1 2027; design: children/adolescents aged 8–17.5, randomized double-blind placebo-controlled, three dose levels vs placebo 1:1:1:1, 12 weeks; FDA Orphan Drug and Rare Pediatric Disease designations (Priority Review Voucher eligibility) — [Prader-Willi News (delay)](https://praderwillinews.com/news/phase-2-trial-nasal-spray-prader-willi-hunger-delayed-2027/); [Prader-Willi News (planning)](https://praderwillinews.com/news/pws-clinical-trial-planned-2026-test-tnx-2900-nasal-spray-using-oxytocin/); [Tonix TNX-2900 page](https://www.tonixpharma.com/tnx-2900/); [Patient Worthy](https://patientworthy.com/2025/11/23/tonix-advances-novel-oxytocin-treatment-for-prader-willi-syndrome-into-clinical-testing/)
- Acadia's Phase 3 COMPASS PWS (intranasal carbetocin, ACP-101; ages 5–30; 12 weeks) missed the primary endpoint (change in Hyperphagia Questionnaire for Clinical Trials at week 12) with no separation on any secondary endpoint; safety consistent with prior trials; announced September 2025; program discontinued — [FPWR blog](https://www.fpwr.org/blog/phase-3-compass-pws-trial-of-intranasal-carbetocin-does-not-meet-primary-endpoint); [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/acadia-pharmaceuticals-phase-3-compass-pws-trial-misses-primary-endpoint); [Global Genes RARE Daily](https://globalgenes.org/raredaily/acadias-intranasal-therapy-for-pws-fails-in-phase-3-study/)
- Biological rationale: individuals with PWS have ~42% fewer oxytocin-producing neurons in the hypothalamic paraventricular nucleus — [PMC11353121](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11353121/)
- FPWR maintains an oxytocin project tag and lay summaries of funded trials (e.g., an 8-week double-blind RCT with 23 children/adolescents; Miller et al. pilot) — [FPWR oxytocin projects](https://www.fpwr.org/fpwr-funded-projects/tag/oxytocin); [FPWR publication summary](https://www.fpwr.org/pws-research-publications/intranasal-oxytocin-versus-placebo-for-hyperphagia-and-repetitive-behaviors-in-children-with-prader-willi-syndrome-a-randomized-controlled-pilot-trial)
- Tonix's magnesium-potentiation rationale: Mg2+ potentiates oxytocin at its receptor in animal models and reduces "paradoxical high-dose inhibition," which Tonix argues improves consistency of effect — [Tonix press release via SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1430306/000183988223028167/ex99-01.htm)

### Inferences
- The TNX-2900 Phase 2 start in Q1 2027 implies topline data no earlier than 2028, so within the 36-month window the PWS catalyst is trial initiation and enrollment news, not efficacy.
- FPWR and Prader-Willi News already publish timely, accurate trial coverage; a generalist site cannot compete on news but could add value with a cross-indication explainer (PWS vs AVP-D vs autism: why oxytocin keeps failing at high dose and whether low-dose/potentiated approaches change that).

### Gaps
- PWSA USA's specific oxytocin content was not surfaced (only FPWR).
- Whether Tonix has stated the reason for the delay to Q1 2027 was not visible in snippets.

## KQ4. Psychiatry / psychotherapy augmentation

### Takeaway
The transdiagnostic evidence for standalone intranasal oxytocin is weak-to-null (2026 autism and alcohol meta-analyses are null or mixed), but the live frontier has shifted to oxytocin as an adjunct to relational or group psychotherapy (VA couples therapy for PTSD, Charité group therapy for schizophrenia), with several readouts in late 2026–2027; no company was found pursuing "oxytocin-assisted therapy" commercially.

### Cited Findings
**Meta-analytic signal**
- Autism, 2026 (Alpha Psychiatry): 12 RCTs, 733 participants; random-effects pooling showed no statistically significant effect of oxytocin on social functioning — [IMR Press PDF](https://storage.imrpress.com/IMR/2083018748025872400/application/2757-8038-27-4-45322.pdf)
- Autism, 2026 (Review Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders): 54 preclinical + clinical studies, 2,593 cases; reported significantly lower least-squares mean change in autism behavioral symptoms with oxytocin vs placebo — [Kocaeli University record](https://avesis.kocaeli.edu.tr/yayin/cbf63890-98ac-4cff-b908-dfbab554f566/intranasal-oxytocin-for-patients-with-autism-spectrum-disorder-a-comprehensive-meta-analysis-of-preclinical-and-clinical-studies). This conflicts with the Alpha Psychiatry result; the latter mixes animal and human data.
- An AAN 2026 abstract also reports a systematic review/meta-analysis of chronic intranasal oxytocin in autism — [AAN 2026](https://index.mirasmart.com/AAN2026/PDFfiles/AAN2026-004551.html)
- A 2025 APS convention paper described itself as the first meta-analysis of clinical outcomes across psychiatric diagnoses since 2013; search summary indicated mixed effects with some suggestion that doses of 48 IU/day or more are more beneficial — [APS 2025](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/conventions/archive/2025annual/paper/54623/)
- Schizophrenia negative symptoms: the most recent full meta-analysis found (Sabe et al. 2021, 9 RCTs) showed no overall effect; a moderate effect at >40–80 IU disappeared after excluding one outlier; dose-response modeling predicted higher doses may be more efficacious — [PMC8378078](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8378078/). No 2025–2026 schizophrenia meta-analysis was found.
- Alcohol use disorder, 2026 (Psychoneuroendocrinology): Bayesian/multilevel meta-analysis concluded intranasal oxytocin "does not confer a consistent therapeutic benefit across alcohol-related outcomes," with "context-sensitive signals" in social-cognitive domains — [drugsandalcohol.ie](https://www.drugsandalcohol.ie/46367/)
- Multisite US RCT of intranasal oxytocin for AUD (NCT03878316; UCLA, Johns Hopkins, Boston Medical Center, UVA; 35 IU twice daily = 70 IU/day) — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03878316)

**Psychotherapy-augmentation trials**
- NCT06194851 (VA San Diego; PI Leslie A. Morland, PsyD): randomized placebo-controlled trial of Brief Cognitive-Behavioral Conjoint Therapy (bCBCT) + intranasal oxytocin vs bCBCT + placebo in veterans with PTSD and their partners; outcomes PTSD symptoms, relationship satisfaction, psychosocial functioning; mechanisms explored: communication, empathy, trust — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06194851); [UCSD listing](https://clinicaltrials.ucsd.edu/trial/NCT06194851)
- A predecessor protocol (NCT05207436) for bCBCT with adjunctive intranasal oxytocin is published — [protocol PDF](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/36/NCT05207436/Prot_002.pdf)
- Alcohol Behavioral Couple Therapy + 40 IU oxytocin, RCT in 96 dyads (49 OT / 47 placebo), JCP May 2025: oxytocin safe and tolerable but no added benefit over therapy alone — [Journal of Clinical Psychiatry](https://www.psychiatrist.com/jcp/randomized-controlled-trial-intranasal-oxytocin-enhance-alcohol-behavioral-couple-therapy)
- COPE+OT (oxytocin to enhance integrated AUD/PTSD treatment in veterans) listed as completed July 2026 — [tundraspace NCT05432089](https://tundraspace.com/directory/studies/nct05432089) (NCT mapping from snippet; verify)
- OXYMIND2.0 (NCT07265180, Charité Berlin): oxytocin-augmented mindfulness-based group psychotherapy for negative symptoms in schizophrenia spectrum disorders, with an oxytocin dose comparison; started September 2025; recruiting; estimated completion November 2026; protocol published in European Psychiatry (Aug 2025) — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07265180); [European Psychiatry protocol](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/european-psychiatry/article/oxytocinaugmented-group-psychotherapy-for-negative-symptoms-in-patients-with-schizophrenia-spectrum-disorders-a-study-protocol/177B40D2CDFC984F2589A2FF07E5E357)
- A randomized pilot giving oxytocin to both patient and therapist to study physiological synchronization (120 participants, March 2023–July 2026) — [Frontiers in Psychiatry 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2025.1671944/pdf) (attribution of the synchrony study to this PDF is from snippet summary; verify)
- Concordia University: 24 patients with MDD, 16 weeks psychotherapy + intranasal oxytocin or placebo; cortisol awakening response became more pronounced with oxytocin but was not sustained at 6 months — [Frontiers in Psychiatry 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2025.1671944/pdf)

### Inferences
- The field's own meta-analyses now argue against oxytocin as a standalone psychiatric drug and toward (a) higher/consecutive-day dosing and (b) pairing with a social context (therapy, couples). That reframing is not reflected in consumer content, which still treats oxytocin as a "love hormone spray."
- The VA trial is the highest-visibility US catalyst in this cluster (veteran PTSD + relationships), and the completion of COPE+OT in July 2026 suggests a publication in 2027.
- No company pursuing oxytocin-assisted psychotherapy as a product was found; the space is academic/VA-funded.

### Gaps
- NCT06194851 estimated completion date not captured.
- MDMA-vs-oxytocin head-to-head comparisons and any "oxytocin as MDMA-lite" discourse were not researched (budget exhausted).
- Full details of the 2025/2026 APS transdiagnostic meta-analysis (authors, effect sizes, journal publication) are not available from snippets.

## KQ5. Metabolic / obesity after the GLP-1 era

### Takeaway
Oxytocin for general obesity has been eclipsed by GLP-1s, and active work is confined to MGH (adolescent obesity; binge eating; Tonix TNX-1900) and CHOP (hypothalamic obesity, crossover trial no longer recruiting); the defensible niche is hypothalamic obesity and PWS, where GLP-1s do not address the underlying hypothalamic deficit.

### Cited Findings
- NCT04551482 (MGH): 12-week RCT of oxytocin vs placebo in adolescents aged 10–18 with obesity, hypothesizing reduced caloric intake and increased energy expenditure partly mediated by reduced hypothalamic inflammation — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04551482)
- CHOP: intranasal oxytocin in hypothalamic obesity (brain-tumor survivors, children through adults), 8 weeks oxytocin vs placebo with 4-week washout and crossover; listed as no longer recruiting — [CHOP](https://www.research.chop.edu/intranasal-oxytocin-in-hypothalamic-obesity); [Penn Medicine listing](https://clinicalresearch.pennmedicine.org/us/en/listing/4992/intranasal-oxytocin-in-hypothalamic-obesity/)
- Tonix states TNX-1900 is in Phase 2 under investigator-initiated INDs at MGH for adolescent obesity, binge eating disorder and bone health in autism, and at University of Washington for social anxiety disorder — [Tonix via Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/975683/tonix-pharmaceuticals-announces-first-participant-dosed-in-phase-1-investigator-initiated-pharmacodynamic-study-of-tnx-1900-intranasal-potentiated-oxytocin-to-assess-potential-for-treating-migraine-and-craniofacial-pain)
- A 2025 Frontiers in Endocrinology article on oxytocin and appetite/satiety in obesity exists — [Frontiers in Endocrinology 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fendo.2025.1708807/full) (content not read)

### Inferences
- No new company entered oxytocin-for-obesity in the surfaced 2025–2026 material; Tonix is the only sponsor and its obesity work is investigator-initiated. Expect readouts (if any) to be small academic publications rather than company catalysts.

### Gaps
- Status/results of NCT04551482 and the CHOP hypothalamic obesity trial were not retrieved.
- Post-GLP-1 positioning statements by investigators (e.g., oxytocin as add-on to semaglutide) were not researched.

## KQ6. Pain, addiction, caregiver stress, menopause/GSM, PSSD, autism, wound healing, bone, cardiovascular/longevity

### Takeaway
Several mid-sized, well-powered trials read out in 2026–2027 (a 336-participant chronic-pain crossover, a 244-participant Phase 3 oxytocin vaginal gel for GSM, a 60-participant benzodiazepine-withdrawal RCT, a dementia-caregiver RCT), and a JAMA Psychiatry RCT (Nov 2025) linked oxytocin plus physical intimacy to faster wound healing; none of these has meaningful consumer-facing explanation.

### Cited Findings
**Pain**
- NCT04903002 (Memorial University of Newfoundland): multisite, placebo-controlled, blinded sequential within-subjects crossover of 24 IU intranasal oxytocin for chronic pain; 336 participants; primary completion March 31, 2026; status "Active, not recruiting" as of Feb 18, 2026 — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04903002)
- Heidelberg: randomized placebo-controlled crossover in chronic low back pain found intranasal oxytocin attenuated heat-pain intensity ratings; a related study showed oxytocin modulates intrinsic neural activity in CLBP — [IASP Pain Research Forum](https://www.iasp-pain.org/publications/pain-research-forum/papers-of-the-week/paper/138451-pain-modulating-effects-oxytocin-patients-chronic-low-back-pain); [Heidelberg working group](https://www.klinikum.uni-heidelberg.de/en/zentrum-fuer-psychosoziale-medizin-zpm/klinik-fuer-allgemeine-psychiatrie/forschung/ag-persoenlichkeitsstoerungen/zentrale-oxytocin-effekte-bei-akutem-und-chronischem-schmerz)
- Tonix TNX-1900 (migraine/craniofacial pain) rationale: oxytocin inhibits CGRP release in trigeminal pathways; Mg2+ eliminates high-dose inhibition so analgesia increases with dose in animal models — [Tonix via Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/975683/tonix-pharmaceuticals-announces-first-participant-dosed-in-phase-1-investigator-initiated-pharmacodynamic-study-of-tnx-1900-intranasal-potentiated-oxytocin-to-assess-potential-for-treating-migraine-and-craniofacial-pain)

**Addiction**
- Benzodiazepine withdrawal, NCT06757517 (St. Olavs Hospital, Norway): pilot RCT, 60 inpatients aged 18–65 on 20–80 mg diazepam-equivalent, Syntocinon 16 IU three times daily (48 IU/day) for 21 days during taper; outcomes withdrawal symptoms, sleep, anxiety, craving; start March 1, 2022; estimated completion Dec 31, 2025 — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06757517); [CTIS 2024-516262-11-00](https://www.ctis.eu/trial/2024-516262-11-00)
- Opioids: Phase 1 trial (Brown; NCT04051619), 80 IU intranasal oxytocin twice daily for 7 days in 20 people on opioid agonist therapy, significantly reduced cue-elicited opioid-like withdrawal and anxiety under noradrenergic activation (Biological Psychiatry: GOS, Jan 2025) — [DOAJ record](https://doaj.org/article/1ead01412a0745cda2532d1da3585da3); [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04051619)
- NCT05761860 (University of Florida): oxytocin to reduce opioid abuse liability and pain in older adults (oxycodone +/- oxytocin, 6 lab sessions); primary completion Dec 2026, overall July 2027 — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05761860); [UF Health](https://ufhealth.org/clinical-trials/effects-of-oxytocin-and-oxycodone-on-decision-making-and-pain)
- Alcohol: see KQ4 (2026 meta-analysis null/mixed; couples-therapy RCT null).

**Caregiver stress**
- NCT06364228 (University of Nebraska): RCT of 12 IU vs 24 IU intranasal oxytocin vs placebo daily for 21 days in caregivers of persons with dementia; outcomes quality of life and chronic stress — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06364228)

**Menopause / GSM**
- NCT06514586: Phase 3 double-blind RCT of topical vaginal oxytocin gel vs placebo in postmenopausal women with vulvovaginal atrophy; 244 planned participants — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06514586)
- 2023 systematic review/meta-analysis of oxytocin gel in vaginal atrophy; a 600 IU/mL intravaginal oxytocin gel counteracted physical signs of atrophy in a 2023 trial — [PMC10505316](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10505316/); [SLU](https://research.slu.se/en/publications/effect-of-topical-oxytocin-gel-on-vaginal-mucosa-in-postmenopausa/)
- OxyMENO (Basel, 2026–2027) tests whether postmenopausal women have lower cumulative neurophysin-I — see KQ1 — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07742124)

**PSSD**
- Literature review: decreased oxytocin is implicated as a mechanism of SSRI-induced delayed ejaculation; 5-HT1A receptor downregulation with downstream effects on neurosteroid and oxytocin systems is one of several proposed PSSD mechanisms — [PubMed 28778697](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28778697/); [SAGE 2025](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/26318318251414629)

**Autism** — see KQ4 (2026 null meta-analysis in Alpha Psychiatry; conflicting mixed-species meta-analysis).

**Wound healing / dermatology**
- JAMA Psychiatry (Nov 2025) RCT in 80 romantic couples: daily intranasal oxytocin plus instructed positive interaction enhanced healing of dermatological wounds, especially with daily physical intimacy — [PMC12613093](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12613093/); [PubMed 41222549](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41222549/)
- Dermatology Times covered correlational data linking higher oxytocin to lower skin-age scores and an "Oxytocin Social Exchange System" framework — [Dermatology Times](https://dermatologytimes.com/view/oxytocin-and-its-effect-on-skin-aging)
- NCT05745935: local skin-incision infiltration with oxytocin for wound healing — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05745935)

**Bone**
- 2024 systematic review: 30 studies (19 animal, 11 human); animal data show oxytocin promotes bone formation and inhibits resorption; lower blood oxytocin observed in women with osteoporosis vs healthy same-age controls — [Med J Islam Repub Iran 2024](https://mjiri.iums.ac.ir/article-1-9016-en.html)
- Brazilian (FAPESP) rodent work suggests oxytocin given during perimenopause may prevent osteoporosis; authors hope to move to human trials — [Agência FAPESP](https://agencia.fapesp.br/oxytocin-can-help-prevent-osteoporosis/33845)
- 2025 review in Women (MDPI) on oxytocin's anabolic bone effects in postmenopausal osteoporosis — [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-4184/5/3/27)

### Inferences
- The chronic-pain crossover (n=336) is the largest controlled intranasal oxytocin trial surfaced in this research and, with primary completion March 2026, a publication is plausible in late 2026–2027; a positive result would be the first large-trial efficacy signal for oxytocin outside obstetrics.
- The GSM gel trial (n=244) is a Phase 3 and, if positive, could create a non-hormonal/"hormone-adjacent" alternative narrative for women avoiding estrogen—an audience with high search intent.
- Bone and PSSD remain preclinical/mechanistic with no human trial found; cardiovascular/longevity claims were not substantiated by any surfaced trial.

### Gaps
- Cardiovascular and longevity claims: not researched (budget exhausted); no sources.
- Results for NCT06757517 (estimated completion Dec 2025) were not found.
- Sponsor/location of NCT06514586 (GSM Phase 3) not captured in snippets.
- US population sizes for chronic pain, GSM, caregivers were not retrieved.

## KQ7. Delivery science: nose-to-brain debate, new formulations, oral/sublingual claims

### Takeaway
The live formulation questions are (1) whether intranasal oxytocin reaches the human brain directly (converging animal/CSF evidence, but human proof still limited), (2) dose non-monotonicity ("high-dose inhibition") that Tonix addresses with magnesium and Biohaven with a long-acting formulation; no source explaining this to consumers was found.

### Cited Findings
- Quintana et al. (2021) review: rodent and non-human primate studies show labeled intranasal oxytocin reaching amygdala and hippocampus via olfactory/trigeminal pathways and functionally relevant CSF increases, but "the extent to which intranasally administered oxytocin reaches the human brain remains uncertain" and direct evidence of central uptake in humans is limited — [PMC7815514](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7815514/)
- A 2025 Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews article from Ruhr-University Bochum medical psychology addresses intranasal oxytocin (content not read) — [Bochum PDF](https://www.medpsych.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/medpsych/mam/1-s2.0-s0149763425003513-main.pdf)
- Tonix: magnesium potentiates oxytocin at the receptor and reduces "paradoxical high-dose inhibition"; adding Mg2+ to intranasal oxytocin made analgesia dose-dependent in animal head-pain models — [Tonix press release (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1430306/000183988223028167/ex99-01.htm); [Tonix via Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/975683/tonix-pharmaceuticals-announces-first-participant-dosed-in-phase-1-investigator-initiated-pharmacodynamic-study-of-tnx-1900-intranasal-potentiated-oxytocin-to-assess-potential-for-treating-migraine-and-craniofacial-pain)
- Biohaven BHV-1955 is explicitly a "long-acting intranasal formulation" — [Biohaven 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001935979/000193597926000066/bhvn-20260630.htm)
- Dose heterogeneity across trials surfaced here: 6 IU and 24 IU (FOCUS), 12/24 IU (caregivers), 24 IU (pain, OxyPLEASURE), 40 IU (couples therapy), 48 IU/day (benzodiazepine), 70 IU/day (AUD), 80 IU BID (opioid) — sources as cited in KQ1, KQ4, KQ6.
- Meta-analytic hints of a dose threshold: schizophrenia effect only at >40–80 IU (fragile) and a transdiagnostic analysis pointing to ≥48 IU/day — [PMC8378078](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8378078/); [APS 2025](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/conventions/archive/2025annual/paper/54623/)

### Inferences
- The two commercial programs (Tonix, Biohaven) both exist because plain oxytocin spray has an inconsistent dose-response and short duration; "why oxytocin trials keep failing and what formulation fixes are being tried" is a coherent explainer topic with no consumer coverage found.

### Gaps
- Oral/sublingual oxytocin and consumer "oxytocin supplement" claims were not researched (budget exhausted); no evidence gathered.
- Device-based delivery (precision olfactory delivery devices) was not researched.
- The 2025 Bochum NBR review's conclusions on nose-to-brain transport were not read.

## KQ10. Where is momentum highest and coverage lowest? (Synthesis)

### Takeaway
Ranked by (evidence momentum × readout density in 12–36 months) ÷ (existing consumer coverage), the top five whitespace areas are: (1) oxytocin deficiency as a diagnosable endocrine condition (AVP-D/hypopituitarism/craniopharyngioma, with menopause as an expansion hypothesis); (2) tinnitus (Biohaven BHV-1955); (3) chronic pain (n=336 crossover reading out 2026); (4) oxytocin as a psychotherapy/couples-therapy adjunct (VA PTSD, Charité schizophrenia); (5) GSM vaginal oxytocin gel Phase 3 and the broader menopause–oxytocin thread.

### Cited Findings
- Cluster of oxytocin-deficiency readouts: OxyPLEASURE Dec 2026, PHOENIX Feb 2027, OxyMENO June–Sept 2027, FOCUS ongoing since Oct 2025 — [NCT06808516](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06808516); [NCT07361263](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07361263); [NCT07742124](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07742124); [FOCUS](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/35605030/tonix-pharmaceuticals-announces-first-patient-dosed-in-the-investigator-initiated-focus-study-of-tonixs-intranasal-potentiated-oxytocin-in-avp-d-conducted-by-massachusetts-general-hospital)
- Only patient-facing oxytocin-deficiency content found: UK Pituitary Foundation recruitment post — [pituitary.org.uk](https://www.pituitary.org.uk/research-opportunity-oxypleasure-study/)
- Tinnitus: BHV-1955 preclinical, long-acting intranasal, rights acquired Q1 2026; prior human evidence limited to a 15/16-patient pilot — [Biohaven 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001935979/000193597926000066/bhvn-20260630.htm); [PMC5613090](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5613090/)
- Chronic pain: 336-participant crossover, primary completion Mar 2026 — [NCT04903002](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04903002)
- Psychotherapy adjunct: VA bCBCT+OT trial; OXYMIND2.0 completion Nov 2026; COPE+OT completed July 2026 — [NCT06194851](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06194851); [NCT07265180](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07265180); [tundraspace](https://tundraspace.com/directory/studies/nct05432089)
- GSM: Phase 3 oxytocin gel, n=244 — [NCT06514586](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06514586)
- Counter-signal (where momentum is NOT): autism (2026 null meta-analysis) and alcohol (2026 null/mixed meta-analysis), PWS (Phase 3 carbetocin failure; mature community coverage) — [IMR Press](https://storage.imrpress.com/IMR/2083018748025872400/application/2757-8038-27-4-45322.pdf); [drugsandalcohol.ie](https://www.drugsandalcohol.ie/46367/); [FPWR](https://www.fpwr.org/blog/phase-3-compass-pws-trial-of-intranasal-carbetocin-does-not-meet-primary-endpoint)

### Inferences
- Area 1 (oxytocin deficiency) has the best combination: a named disease entity being created in real time, two charismatic academic groups, a biomarker (neurophysin-I) and a diagnostic-test trial (PHOENIX), five readouts in the window, and essentially zero US consumer explainers. Patient population is small (tens of thousands in the US) but highly motivated and currently unserved; the OxyMENO menopause hypothesis is the optionality that could expand demand to millions.
- Area 2 (tinnitus) has the largest population and a public-company sponsor whose every filing creates a news hook, but efficacy data will not arrive before ~2028; content now should be expectation-setting (what is known, what BHV-1955 is, why the NYU trial stopped).
- Area 3 (chronic pain) could produce the first large positive or negative controlled result for intranasal oxytocin; either outcome is newsworthy and uncovered.
- Area 4 (therapy adjunct) is the only psychiatry sub-area where the trial design matches the mechanism the meta-analyses now favor (social context + oxytocin); veteran/PTSD framing gives US relevance.
- Area 5 (GSM/menopause) ties a Phase 3 topical product to Basel's postmenopausal neurophysin-I hypothesis; this is the only area where a topical (non-nasal) oxytocin product could reach consumers.
- Low-priority for a new site: PWS (well covered), autism and alcohol (evidence turned null), bone/PSSD/longevity (no human trials).

### Gaps
- Consumer-coverage assessment relied on absence of forum/explainer hits in search results and the composition of top results (which were dominated by registry and journal pages); a proper audit (Google Trends, Reddit, YouTube, TikTok) was not possible within the budget.
- Dates are registry estimates and were not cross-checked against sponsor communications except where cited (Tonix PWS delay; FOCUS first dosing).
