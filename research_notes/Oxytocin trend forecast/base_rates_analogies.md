## Comparable 1: DHEA (1990s anti-aging "superhormone")

### Takeaway
DHEA went from FDA-banned (1985) to OTC supplement (DSHEA 1994) to a ~$50M/yr US market by the late 1990s; definitive null trials (DHEAge 2000; Mayo/NEJM 2006) and three failed Senate attempts to schedule it (2005-2008) neither killed nor grew the market. Thirty years on it is a durable, boring, low-growth supplement line. Pattern: cheap + OTC + null science = survives at modest scale.

### Cited Findings
- FDA banned DHEA sale in 1985; DSHEA (1994) allowed its return as a dietary supplement — [Active.com retrospective](https://www.active.com/articles/dhea-the-anti-aging-pill-that-got-away?cmp=1747)
- Late-1990s buyers were mostly men over 60; ads used slogans like "Stop aging now!" and "fountain of youth" — [PubMed 9555138](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9555138); [NutritionFacts](https://nutritionfacts.org/2023/12/20/dhea-what-is-it-and-what-are-its-benefits/)
- US DHEA sales grew to >$50M/yr; $47M in 2003 — [Active.com](https://www.active.com/articles/dhea-the-anti-aging-pill-that-got-away?cmp=1747)
- Natrol's 1996 revenue growth (+78%) was driven by melatonin and DHEA together — [FundingUniverse Natrol history](https://www.fundinguniverse.com/company-histories/natrol-inc-history/)
- DHEAge study (2000) framed DHEA as a "sociobiomedical issue" — [DHEAge, PNAS 2000](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC18228)
- Mayo Clinic 2-year RCT (87 men, 57 women; NEJM Oct 2006): "no beneficial effects on age-related changes in body composition and function"; no change in body composition, physical performance, insulin sensitivity or QoL; small BMD effect — [NutraIngredients 2006](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2006/10/19/DHEA-supplements-have-no-effect-on-aging-markers-says-study); [MDedge](https://www.mdedge.com/content/dhea-testosterone-show-no-antiaging-benefits)
- DHEA was explicitly exempted from the 2004 Anabolic Steroid Control Act; DEA cannot schedule it because it fails the scheduling criteria; Grassley/McCain/Allen bill S.1137 (2005) and a 2007 bill to classify it as an anabolic steroid both failed — [Grassley statement](https://www.grassley.senate.gov/news/news-releases/prepared-statement-senate-judiciary-subcommittee-hearing-body-building-products); [Congressional Record May 26 2005](https://www.congress.gov/109/crec/2005/05/26/151/72/modified/CREC-2005-05-26-pt1-PgS6030-2.htm); [Pink Sheet 2008](https://pink.pharmaintelligence.informa.com/articles/2008/01/07/grassleys-swing-at-illegal-anabolic-steroid-use-unfairly-strikes-dhea--crn)
- Current market size: market-report estimates range from "$600M (2024, DHEA capsules)" to "$1.15B (2024)" — LOW CONFIDENCE, internally inconsistent — [WiseGuy](https://www.wiseguyreports.com/reports/dhea-capsules-market); [Strategic Revenue Insights](https://www.strategicrevenueinsights.com/industry/dehydroepiandrosterone-dhea-supplement-market)

### Inferences
- Null trials did not kill DHEA; the decisive factor for survival was OTC status via DSHEA plus the failure of scheduling bills. Growth stalled, but a 30-year tail exists.
- Oxytocin differs on the key durability feature here: it is Rx-only, not DSHEA-eligible.

### Gaps
- Reliable current US DHEA retail sales (SPINS/Nielsen) not found; market-report figures are unreliable.
- No source on fate of 1990s DHEA content/affiliate sites.

## Comparable 2: HGH anti-aging clinics (2000s)

### Takeaway
HGH is the one US drug that cannot legally be prescribed off-label (1990 statute, 5-10 yr penalties), yet an illegal anti-aging clinic industry grew through the 2000s (279 clinic websites by 2006; 549 Florida clinics by 2013) and was only episodically dented by federal raids (2007 Operation Which Doctor, 2014 Biogenesis). Today the same clinic ecosystem has migrated to "legal-ish" GH secretagogues (sermorelin, ipamorelin) sold via telehealth at $99-400/month. Pattern: criminal enforcement suppresses but does not eliminate; the ecosystem substitutes the molecule.

### Cited Findings
- HGH marketed 1985 for pediatric growth failure; 1990 law limited distribution to specific conditions, overriding normal off-label prescribing; prescribing for weight loss/strength punishable by 5-10 years — [AP via FiftyPlus Advocate 2013](https://www.fiftyplusadvocate.com/2013/02/08/fountain-of-youth-treatment-has-dangerous-side-effects/); [MPR News/AP 2013](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2013/01/02/big-pharma-cashes-in-on-hgh-abuse)
- 2006: 279 anti-aging clinic websites advertising HGH and 26 distributing pharmacies; 2013: Florida had 549 anti-aging clinics — [Health News Florida 2013](https://health.wusf.usf.edu/health-news-florida/2013-12-18/doh-ignores-anti-aging-scams-report)
- 2007 "Operation Which Doctor" raided Signature Pharmacy (Orlando); Aug 2014 charges against Biogenesis of America, Biokem, Revive Miami; Biogenesis founder sentenced Feb 2015 — [DEA press release 2015](https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2015/02/17/biogenesis-founder-sentenced-today-his-role-conspiracy-distribute); [Daily Herald 2013](https://www.dailyherald.com/20130101/business/feds-fail-to-stop-rampant-steroid-use/)
- Illegal anti-aging use "probably accounts for the largest use of growth hormone in the United States"; anti-aging sales estimated at $2B/yr (ESTIMATE, undated) — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/health/health-news/call-crackdown-docs-who-peddle-hgh-flna1c9462738); [The Hospitalist](https://blogs.the-hospitalist.org/content/risks-outweigh-benefits-using-hgh-antiaging)
- 2025-26: sermorelin/ipamorelin sold via telehealth platforms (Sermorelin.com, ReadyRx) at $99-$400/month with a prescription; "sermorelin alternative" searches claimed +340% YoY (press release, LOW CONFIDENCE) — [Santelog/ReadyRx PR](https://www.santelog.com/actualites-sante-nasdaq/readyrx-sermorelin-prescription-telehealth-access-injectable-and-odt-formats); [Peptides Explorer](https://peptidesexplorer.com/blog/where-to-buy-sermorelin)

### Inferences
- The "anti-aging clinic" is the durable unit, not the molecule. The clinic base that sold HGH in 2005 sells testosterone, sermorelin, GLP-1s and peptides in 2026. Oxytocin is a plausible add-on menu item for this ecosystem rather than a standalone trend.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 count of HGH-specific clinics or enforcement actions found.

## Comparable 3: Testosterone / TRT (2010s-2026)

### Takeaway
TRT is the clearest "boom, regulatory/safety trough, rebound" case: prevalence peaked 0.83% (2013), fell to 0.64% (2021) after the 2015 FDA label warning (-48% existing users, -62% new users 2013-16), then rebounded to 0.95% by mid-2026 after TRAVERSE (2023) and the Feb 2025 removal of the boxed CV warning. The rebound is telehealth-led (Hone $113.5M ARR Sept 2025; Hims entering 2026). Pattern: approved generic + felt effect + favorable big trial = durable, now larger than the original peak.

### Cited Findings
- Prescription prevalence peaked at 0.83% in 2013, fell to 0.64% in 2021, rose from ~2022 to 0.93% in 2025 and 0.95% by mid-2026 — [Epic Research](https://www.epicresearch.org/articles/after-nearly-a-decade-long-decrease-testosterone-prescribing-rates-rising-again/)
- March 2015 FDA labeling change (not approved for age-related low T; possible MI/stroke risk); 2013-2016: -48% established users, -62% new users — [TAU editorial](https://tau.amegroups.org/article/view/25241/html); [Medical Dialogues](https://speciality.medicaldialogues.in/amp/fearing-side-effects-sharp-decline-in-testosterone-prescriptions)
- Feb 2025: FDA removed the boxed CV warning after TRAVERSE (2023) found no increased MACE — [US Pharmacist June 2025](https://www.qgdigitalpublishing.com/article/TRENDWATCH/4986299/847077/article.html); July 2026: Trump FDA moving to make testosterone easier to prescribe — [Fortune July 16 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/07/16/testosterone-access-fda-trump-policy-shift/)
- 2022 study: only 1 in 7 online TRT prescribers asked basic screening questions — [Courthouse News](https://courthousenews.com/trump-officials-want-to-make-testosterone-drugs-easier-to-prescribe-is-that-a-good-idea/)
- Hone Health ARR $113.5M (Sept 2025) up from $69.7M (end 2024); $129-149/month subscription; Hims ($2B TTM revenue) launching TRT in 2026 via Marius oral testosterone partnership, already selling compounded enclomiphene at $99/month — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/hone-health/); [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/hims-enters-testosterone-market--expanding-its-d2c-health-footprint)

### Inferences
- TRT is the best-case reference class and oxytocin shares almost none of its features: TRT has a felt effect, measurable biomarker (serum T), an approved reimbursable product, and a large positive outcome trial. Oxytocin has none of these.

### Gaps
- Absolute US TRT prescription counts (vs. Epic prevalence %) not found.

## Comparable 4: Melatonin (1995 "miracle hormone" to normalized supplement)

### Takeaway
Melatonin is the archetype of a hormone fad that normalized: an Aug-Nov 1995 media spike (two Newsweek covers; stores rationing bottles) was followed by a trough, then slow mainstreaming to a $820M US market (2020), 2.1% adult prevalence (2017-18) and 64% ever-use (AASM survey). The decisive factors were OTC status under DSHEA, pennies-per-dose cost, and a sleep effect users can perceive. Safety backlash (pediatric ingestions +530% 2012-21, two deaths) produced advisories, not restriction, in the US; Australia keeps it Schedule 4 Rx-only.

### Cited Findings
- Market "exploded" Aug 1995 with books and broadcasts; Newsweek cover Nov 6, 1995 "The Melatonin Craze"; Miami store limited customers to 20 bottles; Chicago store sales rose 10x in 8 weeks — [NASW ScienceWriters 1996](https://www.nasw.org/sites/default/files/sciencewriters/html/win96tex/melatoni.htm); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/melatonin-mania-181224)
- Natrol revenue +95% in 1995 to $23.6M, melatonin $6.7M (28%) — [FundingUniverse](https://www.fundinguniverse.com/company-histories/natrol-inc-history/)
- US melatonin sales $285M (2016) to $820M (2020), +150% — [Sleep Review](https://sleepreviewmag.com/sleep-treatments/pharmaceuticals/otc/surge-otc-melatonin-use-isnt-just-imagination)
- Adult use prevalence 0.4% (1999-2000) to 2.1% (2017-2018) (JAMA 2022) — [Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/primary-care/20220214/melatonin-use-significantly-increases-across-us); AASM survey: 64% have taken melatonin — [Newswise/AASM](https://www.newswise.com/articles/expert-available-melatonin-use-on-the-rise-among-u-s-adults)
- Pediatric ingestions +530% 2012-2021; >260,000 poison-center cases; ~4,000 hospitalizations; two deaths — [Wayne State Poison Center on CDC MMWR](https://www.poison.med.wayne.edu/updates-content/cdc-report-sees-a-530-increase-in-pediatric-ingestions-of-melatonin); [TIME](https://time.com/6183589/melatonin-poisoning-kids/)
- AASM/ACP guidelines recommend against melatonin for chronic insomnia; AASM health advisory on pediatric use; Australia regulates as Schedule 4 prescription-only — [ChemLinked regulatory overview](https://food.chemlinked.com/expert-article/understanding-the-global-regulatory-environment-for-melatonin-supplements); [KMPH fact check](https://kmph.com/news/nation-world/fact-check-team-experts-sound-alarm-on-high-melatonin-usage-among-children-and-adults-dietary-supplement-health-advisory-american-academy-of-sleep-medicine-gummy-poison-control-center-doctor-hospital-health-care-cdc)

### Inferences
- The anti-aging/immune claims of 1995 died; the sleep use case (felt, cheap, OTC) carried the market. Oxytocin's equivalent "felt" use case does not exist in healthy adults.

- 2024-2025 US melatonin retail sales figure not retrieved (search budget exhausted).

## Comparable 5: Resveratrol (2006-2015)

### Takeaway
Resveratrol had the strongest scientific-institutional backing of any fad here (Nature mouse paper 2006; Sirtris IPO 2007; GSK $720M acquisition April 2008) and collapsed fastest: SRT501 trial halted Dec 2010 after five deaths, Sirtris executives caught selling supplements online (2010), Dipak Das fraud finding Jan 2012 (145 counts), Sirtris shuttered 2013. The US supplement market was only ~$31M (2009)/$50M global (2012) even at peak. Pattern: pharma-pipeline failure + fraud scandal + <1% bioavailability = hype dies, small supplement niche persists.

### Cited Findings
- Sinclair's Harvard group showed resveratrol extended lifespan of obese mice; Sirtris IPO ~6 months later; GSK bought Sirtris for $720M April 2008 — [New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/article/112706)
- Dec 2010: SRT501 Phase II in multiple myeloma terminated; five patient deaths from renal failure — [SingularityHub 2011](https://singularityhub.com/2011/01/17/gsk-drops-resveratrol-could-the-embattled-supplement-live-on-in-other-forms/)
- Aug 2010: GSK ordered Sirtris founders Westphal and Dipp to stop selling resveratrol via Healthy Lifespan Institute — [WBUR](https://www.wbur.org/news/2010/08/13/execs-in-hot-seat-gsk-orders-end-to-resveratrol-sales-online); [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/gsk-orders-sirtris-execs-to-stop-selling-resveratrol)
- Jan 11, 2012: UConn found Dipak Das guilty of 145 counts of fabrication/falsification; fired May 2012; >20 papers retracted — [Wikipedia: Dipak K. Das](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dipak_K._Das); [MinnPost](https://www.minnpost.com/second-opinion/2012/01/charges-scientific-fraud-add-bizarre-twist-controversial-resveratrol-story/)
- Sirtris shuttered 2013; "no red-wine longevity pills on the horizon" — [Wine Spectator](https://www.winespectator.com/articles/resveratrol-science-hits-a-wall-44717)
- US consumers spent >$31M on resveratrol supplements in 2009; global supply market $50.2M in 2012, 90% US supplements — [NutraIngredients 2012](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2012/06/13/US-dominates-global-resveratrol-market/)
- Human trials consistently failed to replicate animal results; oral bioavailability <1% — [Stacking Years](https://www.stackingyears.com/learn/resveratrol-senolytic); GSK left resveratrol "exclusively to the nutritionals industry" — [Pink Sheet](https://pink.pharmaintelligence.informa.com/PS104827/Glaxo-Shifts-Rx-Resveratrol-Focus-But-Nutritional-Interest-Still-Strong); "cautionary tale" — [Fight Aging 2015](https://www.fightaging.org/archives/2015/07/resveratrol-as-a-cautionary-tale.php)

### Inferences
- The relevant parallel for oxytocin is the bioavailability problem: Leng & Ludwig's estimate that ~0.002% of intranasal oxytocin reaches the CNS (see Comparable 16) is the same category of mechanistic objection that eventually hollowed out resveratrol's credibility.

### Gaps
- Current (2024) resveratrol supplement market figure not retrieved; no source on fate of resveratrol affiliate sites post-2012.

## Comparable 6: NAD+ / NMN (2017-2025)

### Takeaway
NMN is the rare case where a regulatory shutdown was reversed: FDA excluded NMN from the supplement definition (Nov 10, 2022), Amazon delisted it (March 13, 2023), the NPA petitioned (March 2023) and sued (Aug 2024), and FDA reversed (Sept 2025). Meanwhile the category grew around the ban via NR (Niagen) and NAD+ IV clinics (800+ US clinics by Aug 2025, $200-1,500/session). Pattern: strong podcast/longevity ecosystem + multiple substitutable molecules + industry litigation = survives regulation.

### Cited Findings
- Nov 10, 2022: FDA determined NMN excluded from the dietary-supplement definition (drug-preclusion clause) — [Lifespan.io](https://lifespan.io/news/fda-purges-nmn-from-the-list-of-supplements/); [NPA](https://www.npanational.org/nicotinamide-mononucleotide-nmn/)
- Amazon removed NMN products effective March 13, 2023 — [NutraIngredients](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2023/02/16/Amazon-removing-NMN-dietary-supplements-citing-FDA-actions/); [SupplySide SJ](https://supplysidesj.com/supplement-regulations/amazon-bans-nmn-in-dietary-supplements)
- March 2023 NPA/ANH citizen petition; Aug 2024 NPA lawsuit; Sept 2025 FDA reversal citing marketing as a supplement since 2017 — [Venable Oct 2025](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2025/10/fda-declares-nicotinamide-mononucleotide-is); [SupplySide SJ](https://www.supplysidesj.com/supplement-regulations/fda-confirms-that-nmn-can-remain-on-market)
- Niagen IV/injectables in 800+ US clinics (Aug 2025) up from 475+ (Dec 2024); Niagen raised FY2025 net sales outlook to +25-30% — [Niagen Bioscience Q2 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250806870957/en/Niagen-Bioscience-Inc.-Reports-Second-Quarter-2025-Financial-Results-and-Increases-Full-Year-Outlook); [Niagen Oct 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251006758125/en)
- NAD+ market estimated $1.40B (2025) (market report, LOW CONFIDENCE); IV sessions $200-1,500; three in four NMN products failed label claims — [TrendX Insights](https://trendxinsights.com/syndicated-market-research-reports/nad-plus-market/)

### Inferences
- Regulators acted ~5 years after the trend's start (2017 to Nov 2022) and the industry outlasted them within ~3 years. Oxytocin lacks the trade-association muscle (NPA) and substitutable-molecule hedge NMN had.

## Comparable 7: BPC-157 and the peptide wave (2022-2026)

### Takeaway
The peptide wave is the most direct structural analog to oxytocin (injectable/nasal peptides, compounded, podcast-driven, telehealth-sold). FDA placed BPC-157 on the 503A Category 2 "significant safety risk" list in late 2023 (about 1-2 years after the Rogan/Huberman-era spike); the grey market continued regardless; April 15, 2026 FDA removed BPC-157 and 11 other peptides from Category 2, and a July 23-24, 2026 PCAC voted 8-6 (1 abstention) in favor of BPC-157 for 503A bulk-list inclusion (ulcerative colitis). Pattern: regulatory whiplash within ~3 years; trend survived its "ban."

### Cited Findings
- Nov 2023 (per law-firm alert): FDA added BPC-157 to Category 2 under 503A, prohibiting compounding pending review — [DJ Holt Law](https://djholtlaw.com/regulatory-alert-the-legal-status-of-bpc-157-in-compounding-and-clinical-practice/)
- April 15, 2026: FDA removed BPC-157 and 11 other peptides from Category 2; not yet on Category 1, so "regulatory limbo"; PCAC meeting July 23-24, 2026 — [DJ Holt Law April 2026 alert](https://djholtlaw.com/regulatory-alert-fdas-april-2026-update-on-bpc-157-and-ghk-cu/); [FDA Law Blog April 2026](https://thefdalawblog.com/2026/04/fdas-peptide-rally-what-compounders-and-industry-need-to-know-post-2-of-2)
- PCAC voted 8-6 with one abstention in favor of adding BPC-157 — [Good Morning America](https://goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/fda-advisory-committee-votes-add-popular-peptide-bpc-134913891); FDA advisers weighed restrictions on seven peptides as industry pressed for expansion (July 23, 2026) — [KFGO/Reuters](https://kfgo.com/2026/07/23/us-fda-advisers-weigh-restrictions-on-seven-peptides-as-industry-presses-for-expansion/)
- 71% of grey-market peptide samples failed FDA-manufactured standards; 42% failed compounded-drug standards; 15% contained endotoxins; 2% lacked the labeled peptide — [FDA Law Blog](https://thefdalawblog.com/2026/04/fdas-peptide-rally-what-compounders-and-industry-need-to-know-post-2-of-2)
- Analog substitution: "pentadeca arginate" marketed as stabilized BPC-157 analog during the ban — [Superpower guide](https://superpower.com/guides/pentadeca-peptide)

### Inferences
- Oxytocin is better positioned than BPC-157 on one axis: oxytocin is the API of an FDA-approved drug (Pitocin), so 503A compounding from bulk does not depend on bulk-list nomination (INFERENCE from general 503A structure; no source fetched). BPC-157 was never an approved drug and had to fight for list status.
- Peptide telehealth sellers (Superpower, Push Health, Empower) already list oxytocin alongside BPC-157, so oxytocin rides the same channel and inherits the same FDA marketing scrutiny.

### Gaps
- Detailed outcome for the other six peptides at the July 2026 PCAC not retrieved (budget exhausted).

## Comparable 8: Semaglutide/GLP-1 compounding boom and bust (2022-2025)

### Takeaway
The only case where an approved product plus a supply event killed a mass grey market: compounded GLP-1s hit ~80M prescriptions (est.) and ~$1B (2024), then FDA declared the semaglutide shortage over (Feb 2025; 503A stop by April 22, 503B by May 22), Hims stock fell 25% in a day, FDA issued 80+ warning letters to telehealth marketers in the 12 months to March 2026 (30 on March 3, 2026 alone), Hims took a $33M restructuring charge and partnered with Novo (March 2026). Pattern: an accessible approved product + manufacturer litigation + FDA deadline = grey market compressed to a "personalized dosing" remnant within ~14 months.

### Cited Findings
- Est. 80 million compounded GLP-1 prescriptions in 2024; market ~$1B by 2024; compounded price $250-400/month vs ~$1K branded — [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/report/how-glp-1s-broke-healthcare-distribution) (ESTIMATES)
- Feb 2025 FDA declared semaglutide shortage resolved; compounders given until April (503A) / May (503B) 2025 — [Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/health-tech/fda-declares-semaglutide-shortage-over-spelling-end-compounded-glp-1-market); [Pharmaceutical Technology](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/news/semaglutide-shortage-ends-as-fda-sets-deadline-for-compounders/); deadlines April 22 / May 22, 2025 — [Milliman](https://www.milliman.com/en-GB/insight/healthcare-stakeholders-glp-1-compounding)
- Hims shares fell 25% on the shortage-over announcement — [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/well/article/novo-nordisk-wegovy-ozempic-semaglutide-glp-1-shortage-declared-over)
- Sept 2025 FDA Warning Letter to Hims for false/misleading GLP-1 marketing; Dec 2025 warning letter to Hims' 503B facility — [Frier Levitt](https://www.frierlevitt.com/articles/hims-compounded-wegovy-fda-glp1-compounding-enforcement/)
- March 3, 2026: FDA issued 30 warning letters to telehealth companies; 80+ in the 12 months ending March 2026 — [Venable March 2026](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2026/03/fdas-latest-glp-1-crackdown-what-compounders); [FDA Law Blog](https://thefdalawblog.com/2026/04/fdas-peptide-rally-what-compounders-and-industry-need-to-know-post-2-of-2)
- March 2026: Hims ceased advertising compounded GLP-1s and partnered with Novo Nordisk; ~$33M restructuring, $28.5M write-down of compounded GLP-1 supply chain — [Hims 10-Q FY2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001773751/000177375126000163/hims-20260630.htm); [Kavout](https://www.kavout.com/market-lens/hims-hers-s-glp-1-revenue-surge-erodes-core-profitability)

### Inferences
- Regulatory reaction time: ~2.5 years from compounding boom (mid-2022) to shortage declaration (Feb 2025), then ~12 months of marketing enforcement. This is the most relevant precedent for how fast FDA now moves against telehealth marketing of compounded products, and oxytocin sellers are inside that enforcement perimeter.
- Oxytocin has no branded competitor to displace it the way Wegovy/Zepbound displaced compounded GLP-1s, so the "approved product kills grey market" mechanism does not apply.

### Gaps
- No source found on what happened to GLP-1 shortage-tracker/affiliate content sites after Feb 2025 (search budget exhausted before this query ran).

## Comparable 9: Ketamine telehealth (2020-2026)

### Takeaway
Ketamine split into two markets: the approved, reimbursed product (Spravato: $780M in first 9 months of 2024, +62% YoY, on track for >$1B; monotherapy approval Jan 2025) grew steadily, while the generic-ketamine clinic/telehealth wave boomed on COVID DEA flexibilities and partly busted (Ketamine Wellness Centers' 13 clinics closed March 2023; Field Trip CCAA March 2023; Matthew Perry death Oct 2023 with doctors sentenced Dec 2025). DEA has extended telehealth flexibilities repeatedly, now to Dec 31, 2026. Pattern: felt effect + approved reimbursed product = durable core; unreimbursed cash clinics = fragile.

### Cited Findings

## Synthesis 1: Base rates across the comparables

### Takeaway
Across the 13 comparables old enough to score (5+ years, or clearly resolved), roughly 8 (about 60%) still have a durable consumer business today, 2-3 (about 20%) were materially regulated away or compressed by regulation/approved-product displacement, and zero were killed outright by null trials alone. Null trials shrank hype (DHEA, resveratrol, oxytocin-autism) but markets persisted wherever the product stayed cheap and legally accessible. The strongest single predictor of a 5-year consumer market is legal OTC/cosmetic access; the strongest predictor of growth beyond the initial spike is a felt effect plus an approved, reimbursed product.

## Synthesis 2: Which features predicted durability

### Takeaway
Ranked by how consistently they separated survivors from casualties in this set: (1) legal low-friction access (OTC/cosmetic/de facto OTC) - present in every survivor except TRT/ketamine, which substituted (2) an approved product with reimbursement; (3) a felt effect (sleep, mood, libido, energy, weight) - present in melatonin, TRT, ketamine, GLP-1, absent in DHEA, resveratrol, pheromones, PT-141, oxytocin; (4) a strong cultural/sex/attraction hook - sufficient for durability only when combined with (1) (pheromones), insufficient alone (PT-141, kisspeptin); (5) cheap generic availability - necessary but not sufficient; (6) a podcast/celebrity ecosystem - drives the spike height, not the 5-year outcome (resveratrol had Sinclair/60 Minutes and still collapsed; methylene blue TBD); (7) safety scandals - trimmed demand (TRT 2015, ketamine 2023, melatonin pediatric) but never ended a market with features 1-3.

### Cited Findings
- Felt effect + OTC: melatonin $285M to $820M 2016-2020 — [Sleep Review](https://sleepreviewmag.com/sleep-treatments/pharmaceuticals/otc/surge-otc-melatonin-use-isnt-just-imagination)
- Felt effect + approved reimbursed product: Spravato $780M 9M-2024 — [PharmaVoice](https://www.pharmavoice.com/news/jnj-spravato-blockbuster-depression/730988/); TRT prevalence 0.95% mid-2026 — [Epic Research](https://www.epicresearch.org/articles/after-nearly-a-decade-long-decrease-testosterone-prescribing-rates-rising-again/)
- Hook without felt effect, Rx-only, unreimbursed: Vyleesi gross sales $0.8-1.8M/quarter — [Palatin 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/911216/000165495421012130/ptn_ex991.htm)

## Synthesis 3: Classifying oxytocin and naming its reference class

### Takeaway
Oxytocin's feature set (cheap generic API; Rx-only; 503A-compounded only; no reliably felt effect in healthy adults; null pivotal trial and a replication-crisis literature; very strong cultural hook; microcap pipeline; sits inside FDA's active telehealth-marketing enforcement perimeter) most closely matches PT-141/Vyleesi on commercial mechanics and BPC-157 on channel/regulatory exposure, with pheromone perfume as the model for the meme's cultural persistence. The best-fit composite forecast: a durable but small line item on peptide-clinic and telehealth menus (PT-141-scale: low single-digit $M/quarter equivalent), a culturally persistent meme that spawns oxytocin-themed non-drug products (pheromone pattern), and a regulatory risk profile driven by marketing-claim enforcement rather than molecule bans (GLP-1/BPC-157 pattern). It does not resemble TRT, melatonin, ketamine or NAD+, the four cases that grew after their trough.

### Cited Findings
Feature-by-feature mapping (sources in comparable sections):
- Cheap generic: like DHEA, melatonin, PT-141 (compounded), TRT. Not a differentiator.
- Rx-only, compounded 503A: like BPC-157, sermorelin, PT-141 grey market, compounded GLP-1 — the channel FDA sent 80+ warning letters to in 12 months — [Venable](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2026/03/fdas-latest-glp-1-crackdown-what-compounders)
- Unfelt effect: like DHEA (Mayo 2006 null), PT-141, pheromones; unlike TRT, melatonin, ketamine, GLP-1.
- Null/unreplicated trials: NEJM 2021 autism null; Walum 2016; Leng & Ludwig — [Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/primary-care/20211014/oxytocin-does-not-improve-social-functioning-in-children-with-autism-study-finds); [McCullough](https://michael-mccullough.com/social-science-evolving/blog/2015/05/20/human-oxytocin-research-gets-a-drubbing). Closest: DHEA (null RCTs, market flat for 20 years) and resveratrol (mechanistic bioavailability objection).
- Strong cultural hook ("love hormone"): closest to pheromone perfume (30+ years on a debunked premise) — [Iter Intellectus](https://iterintellectus.substack.com/p/the-pheromone-perfume-scam-why-attraction)
- Small biotech pipeline (Tonix Phase 2s): closest to kisspeptin (MVT-602 stalled) and far from Compass (two positive Phase 3s) — [Tonix 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1430306/000199937126005730/tnxp-10k_123125.htm)
- Telehealth/TikTok distribution with self-injection content: like compounded GLP-1 and BPC-157 — [FormBlends/TikTok](https://formblends.com/videos/peptides/peptidejourney-oxytocin)

Outcome of closest comparables at 2 and 5 years:
- PT-141/Vyleesi: 2-yr (2021): gross sales ~$1-1.8M/quarter, rights handed back by launch partner; 5-yr (2024): sold for $12M upfront to a small specialty pharma; grey market present but blocked from routine compounding — [Palatin 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/911216/000165495421012130/ptn_ex991.htm); [GuruFocus](https://m.gurufocus.com/news/1195165/); [CompoundingFinder](https://compoundingfinder.com/medications/pt-141-bremelanotide)

### Inferences
- 2-year forecast by reference class: the compounded-oxytocin market persists as a peptide-clinic/telehealth add-on; the primary downside risk is FDA warning letters over "love hormone"/bonding claims (the GLP-1 marketing precedent), not a molecule-level ban, because oxytocin is an approved-drug API (inference). Expect at least one safety/ethics news event involving self-injection or dating-context use given the TikTok content already circulating.
- 5-year forecast: PT-141-like plateau (small, stable, clinic-menu) is the modal outcome; the DHEA pattern (null science, flat demand, no shutdown) is the second most likely; the TRT/melatonin "normalization and growth" pattern is implausible absent a felt effect or an approved consumer product, and the pipeline (Tonix) cannot supply one in that window.
- For content/affiliate businesses: in every comparable where the grey channel was compressed (GLP-1 compounding, NMN on Amazon), the compression came from a channel event (shortage declaration, marketplace delisting) with 1-3 months' notice; an oxytocin content business should plan for the analogous event to be FDA telehealth-marketing enforcement against its referral partners rather than a loss of the molecule.

### Gaps
- Direct evidence on the fate of affiliate/content sites after a fad's compression (resveratrol post-2012; GLP-1 trackers post-Feb 2025) was not found; the search budget was exhausted before that query ran. The report-writer should treat this as unresearched rather than absent.
- No volume data (search, prescriptions, seller revenue) exists in this note for the 2026 oxytocin wave itself; the only market-size figure found ($385.6M "oxytocin supplement market") is not credible.
